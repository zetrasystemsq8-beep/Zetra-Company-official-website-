    FAQ

    Quick answers.

    What is Zetra?

    Zetra is a Nigerian technology company focused on software development, digital products and automation.

    Which Zetra products are live?

    NaijaLearn, NigerGram and ZetraMail are live. The other Zetra projects are coming soon.

    What is Crucible?

    Crucible is an idea stress-testing app. You enter an idea, it helps you test whether the idea is worth building, and ideas that pass receive a certificate and are imported into Tribunal.

    Can Zetra build software for my organisation?

    Yes. Zetra offers website design and development, custom software, mobile apps, AI-assisted solutions and business automation. Use the contact options on this page to tell us what you need.

    How can I contact Zetra?
