From idea to proof

Where ideas get tested, certified and investigated.

Crucible and Tribunal work together: an idea is stress tested, and the ideas that pass move on to be investigated by experts.

  1. 1

    Submit an idea

    Enter your idea into Crucible.

  2. 2

    Stress test it

    Crucible helps you test whether the idea is worth working on.

  3. 3

    Earn a certificate

    Ideas that pass receive a certificate.

  4. 4

    Imported to Tribunal

    Passed ideas are automatically imported into Tribunal.

  5. 5

    Investigated by experts

    Zetra plans to invite professionals and researchers to investigate them.
